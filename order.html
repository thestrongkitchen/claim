Questions people ask

How much are the meals?

Most are $15 to $18 depending on the dish and the portion style. No membership fee, no minimum order, and delivery is included in our area.

Do I have to subscribe?

No contracts. Order à la carte — nothing to commit to. Order every week, or once and never again.

When will I get my food?

Order by Wednesday at midnight and it arrives that Sunday or Monday. We cook after the deadline, so there is no same-day option.

Is it fresh or frozen?

Fresh, never frozen. Everything is cooked from scratch in our Hamden kitchen that same weekend and keeps in the fridge through the week.

How do I heat it?

Three minutes in the microwave or about fifteen in the oven. It arrives fully cooked in a sealed tray — nothing to prepare.

What if I don't like something?

Tell us and we replace it free. No forms, no argument.
