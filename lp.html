What exactly do I get?

A new menu every week, usually nine or ten dishes. You pick the ones you want — as few or as many as you like. Each one arrives fully cooked in its own sealed container, a single serving, in whichever portion style suits you: Fat Loss, Performance or Protein Plus.

Do I have to sign up for a subscription?

No. Order when you want it and skip when you don't — there's nothing to cancel and no minimum. If you'd rather lock in savings, memberships are easy to join and add extras on top — but they're optional, not the default.

Do you deliver to me?

We deliver to 103 Connecticut towns across New Haven, Hartford and Fairfield counties and beyond. Put your zip in and we'll tell you straight away.

How does delivery work?

Order by Wednesday at midnight and your food arrives that Sunday or Monday, depending on your town. You can also collect it from our kitchen on Sanford Street in Hamden.

How long does the food keep?

It arrives fresh, never frozen, and keeps in the fridge through the week. Heat it in about three minutes in the microwave, or fifteen in the oven.

What if there are things I don't eat?

You pick every meal yourself from that week's menu — nothing is chosen for you. And if a meal isn't for you, tell us and we'll replace it free.
